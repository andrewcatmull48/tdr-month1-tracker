Drinks to enjoy

A handy reminder from your programme guidance.

Still cold drinks

  • Plain water
  • Sugar-free flavoured waters
  • No added-sugar squash
  • Homemade sugar-free ice lollies

Fizzy cold drinks

  • Sparkling water
  • Sugar-free flavoured sparkling waters
  • Diet fizzy soft drinks
  • ‘Zero’ fizzy soft drinks

Hot drinks

  • Filter or instant coffee
  • Tea, herbal teas and spiced teas, e.g. unsweetened masala chai
  • Bovril
  • Hot bouillon
  • Milk/milk alternatives — from 50 ml allowance (excluding full-fat milk)

Use the allowances and guidance given by your own coach if anything differs.

Plans and portions

The random planner is a suggestion tool, not medical advice. Check all plans against your coach’s instructions. You can change any product or quantity.

Stock

Stock counts are based on items in each monthly inventory. Carry-forward copies unused stock into the next month. Expiry dates are reminders only.
